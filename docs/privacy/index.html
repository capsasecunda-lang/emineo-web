Privacy

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no forms.

Controller

The controller of personal data is Emineo, z. s., ID no. (IČO) 29961734, Vítězné náměstí 1145/8, 160 00 Prague 6, Czech Republic. For anything about data protection, write to info@emineo.cz.

Visiting the website

The pages are plain files with no scripts. They store nothing in your browser and load nothing from other websites.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., based in the United States. GitHub records technical data, including IP addresses, to run the service and protect it from abuse. We have no access to these records. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you e-mail us

We use your e-mail address and your message to reply to you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in dealing with your message.

We keep messages only as long as needed and do not pass them on to anyone apart from the e-mail providers that deliver them.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. You can also object to the processing or ask for it to be restricted. Write to info@emineo.cz.

You can also complain to the Office for Personal Data Protection.
