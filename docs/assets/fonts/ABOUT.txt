Fonts used on emineo.cz

  fraunces-roman.woff2, fraunces-italic.woff2
      Fraunces, by Undercase Type and contributors
      https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces

  instrument-sans.woff2
      Instrument Sans, by Rodrigo Fuenzalida and Jordan Egstad for Instrument
      https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-sans

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full licence texts are in
OFL-Fraunces.txt and OFL-InstrumentSans.txt next to this file.

The files here are modified versions of the originals. They were cut down to the characters
and weights this website needs (Latin with Czech and other central European letters) so that
pages load quickly:

  Fraunces           weights 300 to 600, optical size fixed at 72, softness 50, "wonky" forms off
  Instrument Sans    weights 400 to 700, normal width

The fonts are served from this website itself. Nothing is loaded from a font provider.
