About the association

Name

Emineo is Latin for “to stand out” or “to rise above”.

Registration

Emineo, z. s. is an association (spolek) under Czech law, based in Prague. It was entered in the register of associations at the Municipal Court in Prague on 17 September 2026, file no. L 82500.

How it is run

The highest body is the members’ meeting. The statutory body is the chair, Sebastián Minol.

Membership is by written application, approved by the members’ meeting.
