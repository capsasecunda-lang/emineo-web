We support education, culture and personal development.

Emineo, z. s. is a non-profit association based in Prague.

ēmineō Latin: to stand out, to rise above

Purpose

Under our statutes, the purpose of the association is to support education, culture, public awareness and personal development, and related social aims. We work for the benefit of the public, not only our members.

At the moment we are working on apps for learning and personal development.

Any profit can only be used for the association’s activities and administration.
